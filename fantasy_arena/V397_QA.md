## Approved bounded behavior

1. Forecast useful spending, draw first when at least the soul-draw cost remains, then re-evaluate the new hand.
2. Save the coin unless it unlocks a worthwhile newly affordable play; no idle first-turn coin.
3. Take a certain visible automatic-combat lethal before unnecessary spending.
4. Prioritize useful removal or healing against an urgent visible threat.
5. Preserve attack opportunities through existing automatic combat.
6. Reuse legal targeting, including immunity checks.
7. Recompute after removal rather than waste another card on the removed target.
8. Value effective enemy area damage while subtracting friendly losses.
9. Heal genuinely missing HP; hold a useless full-health heal.
10. Buff useful friendly units rather than opponents.
11. Respect hand and board limits.
12. Wait for pending spells and replan after every completed action.

The planner uses its own hand, visible boards/HP/resources and its own deck count. It does not inspect opponent hidden cards, own future deck order, or consume RNG. It is a small greedy heuristic, not a full simulation or strategy search. Existing legality, capacities and automatic attacks are reused. No card, combat, media, layout, or economy rules change.
