- Six shared circular profiles on the same radius, spaced 60 degrees apart.
  Medal faces are orthographic while the stone retains its shallow tilt and depth.
- Official earth/fire/wind/water/dark/light icon pixels provide monochrome height
  only. No official source RGB is used as medal color. Water is the ice crystal.
  All medals share restrained gold, bevels, fixed warm/cool light and relief shading.
- Removed the old painted medallion footprints and the inner bowl geometry.
  Reconstructed a continuous central stone surface; fixed portrait size/position
  and all lobby information, buttons and motion/network code remain unchanged.
- Updated the independent alpha fallback from the same renderer.
- Image read/upload failures select that fallback without uncaught callbacks.
